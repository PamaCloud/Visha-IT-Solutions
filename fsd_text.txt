1. Project Overview
Build a modern, responsive corporate website for Visha IT Solutions. The website must present the company as a professional technology and talent solutions business and generate enquiries for four core services: E-Commerce, Digital Marketing, Recruitment, and Training.
2. Primary Website Goals
Build trust and a professional online presence.
Generate project enquiries and quote requests.
Generate recruitment/hiring enquiries.
Promote training programs and collect candidate enquiries.
Clearly explain the four core services.
Provide easy contact through form, email, phone and WhatsApp if supplied.
Be fast, mobile-friendly, SEO-ready and easy to maintain.
3. Target Users
Business owners and companies looking for e-commerce or digital marketing services.
Companies requiring recruitment or staffing support.
Students and job seekers interested in training programs.
Existing clients looking for support or additional services.

16. Design Requirements
Overall style: modern, clean, corporate and trustworthy.
Primary visual direction: blue/white with dark navy accents.
Rounded cards, clean icons and generous spacing.
Use the supplied reference screenshot as the visual direction.
Use the official Visha IT Solutions logo and brand assets once supplied.
Typography must be readable and consistent.
Animations should be subtle and not reduce performance.
Accessibility: keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, alt text and accessible form labels.
